# F3.03 — physical Warden bolts, headless hit registration and screenshot art review

**Milestone:** Actual moving projectile simulation and enemy HP changes. This is a verified shooting *slice*, not full wave combat, a mobile release, or parity with the Kingshot reference.

## Simulation architecture
- `src/game/HeroBoltCombat.ts`: pure deterministic fixed-60Hz state owner. Each Warden step after the enemy step selects only living enemies within **7.0m**. Target order is explicit: prioritize an enemy within **5m of core**; within a priority band choose the nearest to the Warden, and use stable ID to resolve exact ties.
- Canonical balance drives **0.55-second** firing interval (**33 ticks**), **8 damage** per bolt and **15m/s** three-dimensional travel. Each shot starts at the hero's above-ground launcher and uses authored road-distance motion for a small lead estimate. The projectile remains a real fixed-direction object after launch; there is no invisible instant damage and no retargeted hit after an old target disappears.
- For each flight step, the projectile trajectory is swept against the **moving target's relative 3D segment**, using a fixed collision radius of 0.12m bolt plus 0.43m authored enemy body. This avoids missing a hit merely because either body crosses the collision point between two 60Hz snapshots. The shot may only hit its original target, is removed on the first contact, and expires after 1.25 seconds. Unrelated foes cannot take phantom damage.
- `src/game/EnemyRoadWorld.ts` preserves current HP/hit-tick in immutable snapshots between road ticks. Only positive integer damage to a valid entity ID is permitted; damage is capped at remaining HP, not repeated after defeat. Zero-HP actors **stop moving in a visibly downed state**. Kill resolution, coin drops, removal and reward accounting are deliberately left to F3.04.
- `HeroBoltCombat.sample(alpha)` interpolates rendered bolt position between authoritative 60Hz updates. Aim yaw in the browser eases with a 0.10-second constant. Render updates, tab pause and composition UI cannot drive extra simulation or spawn hits.

## Art and integration
- `src/art/models/HeroBoltVisuals.ts`: original 3D wooden and copper/metal bolt with ivory/gold fletching, plus a short-lived faceted impact star. Both are mesh-validated, cached and uploaded only once, using the established color tokens and lighting. The shot is rendered along its actual physical velocity, not as a separately animated advertising element. No bloom or screen shake.
- Existing `tests/terrain-preview.html?view=movement` retains the same sunlit Fenwall scenery, keyboard movement, camera follow and nineteen deterministic approaching enemies, and adds visible auto-fire, damage-induced hit motion, small nine-tick impact glints and a grounded HUD hit count. The explicit 30-second **preview-only** raider head start remains, so shots can be evaluated immediately without claiming this is a complete playable wave.
- Readability improvement after review: the initial bolt shaft was visually too thin at gameplay zoom; the authored shaft, head, fins and light inlay have been enlarged while preserving its direction, lifetime and real collision math.
