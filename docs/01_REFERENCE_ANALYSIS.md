# Reference analysis — Kingshot advertising (research baseline)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Project reference: *advertised* fast-action tower defense, not Kingshot's actual campaign economy, city simulation, or monetization.

## Exact selected creative and evidence level
**Primary selected video creative:** CreativeScope **KS-02 — A Real-World Prop Opens Into Gameplay**. CreativeScope says this video was observed on YouTube and AdMob, July 22–29, 2026; portrait 360×640; duration 55.38 s. Source: https://www.creativescope.ai/blog/kingshot-ads-july-2026 .

- 0–4 seconds (published time analysis): a hand drops a barrel into the game scene; the field responds.
- 4–18 seconds: the hero attacks; gold scatters; towers appear.
- 18–40 seconds: increasingly numerous enemies and rising counters.
- The publisher reports having opened the original video. This project's author **has NOT independently decoded or watched the entire video file**. This is a documented secondary video inspection and not a frame-accurate first-hand film breakdown. Do not claim otherwise.
- The real-world hand, presenter inserts, branding, dialogue, and promotional claims are **not** gameplay requirements. We preserve the actual fantasy: manipulate a hero, defeat crowds, pick up currency, buy defenses, hold a lane.

**Cross-check creative:** AdMapix Kingshot iOS sample, record prefix `9aef093bdca67ee2`, https://www.admapix.com/blog/ad-intelligence/kingshot-ad-strategy-2026 ; published analysis of contact-sheet frames at 0/2/5/10 seconds. Opening: a small hero beside a curved lane, red attackers further up, numbered coin-cost build pads, green ground and pale snowy cliffs; by 10 seconds a tower and a crowded confrontation. Their investigation explicitly limits interpretation to these four frames, not full video playback. The above may depict another ad variant; do not conflate the two videos.

**Independent gameplay still:** https://media.pocketgamer.com/artwork/na-37474-1761904357/kingshot-tier-list.jpg (blue-and-gold archer between twin bolt towers, red marching units, beige winding lane, build-price markers).

## What the references substantiate
- Third-person/overhead angled camera looking onto a readable isometric-like battlefield.
- Saturated but comparatively restrained stylized color blocks: blue/gold defender versus red opponents, meadow green, beige road, gray stone.
- Visible direct hero movement and combat presented in advertising (actual control scheme cannot be inferred from sampled footage).
- Enemy line / horde, defensive structures, coins, price-marked building positions, player-led immediate upgrades.
- Strong feedback: impact, projectile, coin appearance, enemy deletion, structure appearing.

## What is uncertain / intentionally invented
Do **not** attribute to the ad: exact damage, camera FOV, procedural map generation, AI targeting formulas, save system, enemy types, full wave length, exact animation clips, pathfinding algorithms, boss design, game difficulty or mobile controls. Those are **original design commitments** specified in the other project files.

## Excluded
No Kingshot name or logos in shipped product; no tracing screenshots, copying asset geometry, ripping sound/video, using ad stills as textures, copying characters/king silhouette, monetization, social alliances, timers, paid upgrades, fake playable demonstrations, prestige store or gambling. Original name *Bastionfall* is a **working title only**; clear title/trademark before public release.

## Direct design translation
1. Replace ad's opaque tap logic with consistent WASD/touch hero steering.
2. Replace arbitrary advertised numbers with a deterministic economic rule; player sees costs before committing.
3. Make the 20-second satisfying attack–coin–build sequence a repeatable 20-minute game, not a scripted ad.
4. Keep physically legible units, mathematically reproducible combat, and a defined game-over condition.
5. More enemies create tactical richness, not merely more particles or screen clutter.

## Subsequent research needed
To increase visual fidelity to a **specific** creative, attach/export its full rights-compatible video or captured contact sheet to the repository. Timestamp and classify frames OBSERVED / INTERPRETED / ORIGINAL. Without the full video, never invent a second-by-second storyboard.
