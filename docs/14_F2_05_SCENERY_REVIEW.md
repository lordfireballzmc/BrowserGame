## Design/art implemented

- `src/art/scenery/PropMeshes.ts` defines **five original indexed, flat-shaded, cached rigid meshes**: (1) tapered conical pine with hand-assembled crowns and 0.24m trunk radius, (2) broadleaf tree with 0.27m trunk and three intentionally **spatially separated** faceted ellipsoid leaf masses, (3) three-stone faceted rock outcrop with restrained `stone.base`/`stone.shadow` colors, (4) three crossed triangular blade grass clump, and (5) tiny warm-`ivory` two-face flower with stalk. Grass/corolla faces are double-sided actual triangles to respect backface culling; tree and rock faces share canonical semantic palette slots. No external copyrighted artwork, textures, screen-space glyphs, visual fog or particle illusions are used.
- `src/art/scenery/Scenery.ts` deterministically distributes a fixed **258** decorative instances: **24 pines, 16 broadleaf trees, 42 rock clusters, 152 three-blade grass clumps, and 24 tiny ivory wildflowers**. Five independently salted streams of `SeededRandom` consume stable 32-bit seed-to-uint semantics (no `Math.random`). Each class uses bounded **Poisson-disc-style dart-throw rejection with spatial-hash neighbor testing**; there is an explicit upper bound of 145 attempts per desired item, and the generator **throws** instead of silently underfilling a scene. Seed-v1 observed **1,317 rejected candidates**.
- Placements check the **entire visible footprint**, including eight radial coast samples; an actual **3.5m gap past the road edge** for trees, plus 2.5m beyond all 4×4m build pad squares, full 7×7m keep and western enemy entry, with entire northern riverbed/bank + extra buffer protected. Rocks, grass and flowers have tailored but strict clearance margins; solid tree and rock props cannot overlap. Heights sample `terrainHeight(seed,x,z)` at each accepted world position, not a faked flat Y=0. Trees are constrained to **2.6–5.8m final scaled height**. All decorations are *visual only*: no new collision barriers, enemy routes or gameplay randomization.
- Only **five shared meshes** are uploaded once and rendered as five instanced batches. Instances vary only by deterministic X/Z position, correct seeded Y elevation, Y-rotation and modest scale. CPU caches contain at most three seed-specific placement sets, so switching many seeds cannot grow decoration placement memory without bound.

This is still `tests/terrain-preview.html`, an engineering inspection route, **not the integrated playable stage**.
