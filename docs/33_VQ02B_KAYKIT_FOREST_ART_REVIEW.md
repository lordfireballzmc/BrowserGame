## Actual visual-review findings

**Positive:** fuller, more characterful rounded KayKit tree canopies; noticeably more green tonal variation than older triangular cones; coherent *family relationship* to existing KayKit animated Warden and raiders, shadows anchored to terrain. Larger clustered rocks occupy more screen area; moving the world has not damaged combat clarity or build-pad selection.

**Deficiencies:** several `Rock_1_J` / `Rock_3_Q` clusters read in the actual game like **layered stone mechanisms or collapsed machinery**, not convincing natural organic boulders. This was visible after the final sizing/material adjustment and needs a specific rock-model art replacement, not a generic "more polish" task. The floor is still a flat featureless green ground sheet with very sparse procedural grass, and there is little shrub/undergrowth transitional dressing at the lane edges. Bright blue/gold build pads and castle still look like basic assembled kit components. The keep is partly cropped at the bottom of portrait battle framing; real forest shadows are prominent but scene lighting remains one-note; HUD/graphics selector and portrait labels are engineering-quality rather than a finished fantasy interface. No user art approval, exclusive new sculpting, hardware mobile FPS, thermal or battery measurements.

**Visual-source correction:** initial candidate was **seven simpler Kenney CC0 Nature/Castle Kit models**, accurately source-pinned and already working; inspected real gameplay A/B screenshots exposed too-blocky silhouettes and tiny rocks. **Those candidate files were removed before merge and replaced** with a more cohesive *single KayKit Forest Nature GLB* and correct notices/tests. Do NOT describe the final VQ.02b runtime as seven separate Kenney GLBs or two painted atlases; that is obsolete branch history.
