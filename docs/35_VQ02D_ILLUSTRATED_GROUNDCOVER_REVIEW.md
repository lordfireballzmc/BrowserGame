## Code/source truth

- `GroundcoverPlan.generateGroundcover(seed,original258)` creates **904 visual-only** seeded physical positions: 736 grass clumps (456 from original 152 sites with grouped variants, plus 280 safe meadow clusters), 48 roadside shrubs, 120 yellow/lilac flowers (72 from original 24 sites, plus 48 supplemental). Coastline, riverbank, road lane/shoulders, entry, keep, ten tower pads and existing rocks are protected by original clearance checks. The original 258 F2.05 site positions, physics, enemy routes and gameplay costs remain unchanged.
- `GroundcoverArtLayer` reuses **three source-authored Kay Lousberg KayKit Forest Nature CC0 painted/UV meshes** `Grass_2_D_Color1`, `Bush_3_B_Color1` and `Bush_1_E_Color1` **from the existing already-shipped forest GLB/atlas**; no duplicated large model fetch. The 3D gold and lilac flower sources are genuine **Kenney Nature Kit CC0** free meshes, exact source `Hidencod/tge-assets@3ddcc695adbba9fb918cf31b7c139b3f95b76910`, Git blob SHA `26adf12842ac3cabfc8475e135ae00fbbde49f78` (7,120 bytes) and `9a102fdd587ba2c4c345e20ae1721e4753b556eb` (7,112 bytes). Flowers have PBR **solid-color** stems and petals with no painted atlas; `tests/groundcover.test.ts` audits actual source glTF meshes, hashes and legal data; `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` records original authors. NONEXCLUSIVE source art.
- The 904 copies render in **seven** GPU `InstancedMesh` groups, **421,728 estimated source triangles**. Low grass/flowers do not cast costly large shadows; a few shrubs do. The game stays ONE Three.js/WebGL2 color/depth/shadow scene. `SceneryArtLayer` retains original 152 grass and 24 flower procedural visual fallbacks if optional asset decoding fails; `?understory=legacy` deliberately restores only old grass/flower graphics, keeping camera/road/core/pads/Warden/raider combat identical.
- The full Chrome workflow initially encountered an intermittent test-only `DevToolsActivePort` partial file write (parsed before Chrome flush). `scripts/probe_unified_chrome.mjs` now waits for a valid integer port rather than incorrectly declaring a failed gameplay render. This is included and verified in the final green exact-head run.

## Real art judgment from landscape + portrait before/after

**Positive:** the nearly featureless green field previously displayed dozens of microscopic primitive grass cards. The new game frame has visible sculpted/painted grass groups, deliberate wildflower dots and recognizable leafy bushes at meadow/road edges, with no vegetation inside tower pads or the road, substantially richer from the actual play camera. The first production candidate had 32 conspicuous green spherical bushes (round toy balls); after actual screenshot review this was revised to just **8 smaller rounded accents and 40 more branched/articulated bushes**, with slightly larger lilac/gold flowers. The final screenshots, both orientation A/B, were inspected after the correction.

**Still not studio-quality art:** some source tree canopies and foundation/tower pads are simple circular low-poly primitives, the grassland/road is still largely untextured-looking, the keep is partly offscreen/cropped in portrait battle composition, and debug labels/graphics quality overlays dominate the view. Characters are rigged but still have weak anticipation, punchy recoil, foot grounding and target impact; full tower defense is not playable—no kill crowns, tower purchasing/enemy attacks/win-loss waves. No original-exclusive sculpted fantasy kit, visual user signoff, hardware mobile GPU frame budget/thermals or publicly deployed Pages claimed. No source art or mockup of proprietary Kingdom Rush assets was copied.
