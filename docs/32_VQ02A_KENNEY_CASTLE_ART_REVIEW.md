## Authored-source provenance and actual integration

- Original artist: **Kenney**, [Castle Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit) and [Tower Defense Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/tower-defense-kit), CC0-1.0. Six fully bundled textured glTF/GLB models are reexports from `Hidencod/tge-assets` at **exact source commit `3ddcc695adbba9fb918cf31b7c139b3f95b76910`**. Git blob identity was independently checked byte-for-byte on ingestion and is rechecked by `tests/environment-art.test.ts`; exact models/hashes are in `src/art/assets/EnvironmentManifest.ts`, source credit in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- `src/engine/render/EnvironmentArtLayer.ts` imports embedded-atlas textured source meshes *into the existing Three.js WebGL2 color/depth/shadow scene*; composes a blue-roof central keep with two windowed three-level turrets: **9** castle-piece placements.
- Ten existing terrain-anchored sites each receive a three-piece kit: warm stone border, visibly **raised blue inlay** and gilded placement corners: **30** pad module placements. Locked pads are subdued. All **39** keep/pad module placements use six source models. Original collision, road exclusion, placement coordinates, unlock progression and deterministic game simulation are unchanged.
- `UnifiedThreeRenderer` suppresses the prior procedural *visual* meshes **only after** all six new art files have loaded successfully. On failure the procedural core and pad visuals remain as a fallback. The `?environment=legacy` query is a comparative art mode, not a separate rendering engine or gameplay mode. No new package or runtime CDN requests.

## Critical visual inspection of true before/after frames

**Actual improvement:** the old large empty gray octagonal disks are gone on the primary Three path; screenshot after shows raised deep-blue center inlays, sculpted warm/beige stone borders and gold location accents. The new blue-roofed, visually articulated keepside composition with two higher turrets is more readable and richer than the prior squat procedural keep. Colors coordinate with the blue/gold Warden, and geometry casts/receives real scene shadows. A first production screenshot revealed the azure inset was too deeply buried; its layer height was corrected, verified in the following compiled-site run and new final before/after captures.

**Still under the user's professional 3D reference:** these CC0 Kenney modules are attractive but **generic, relatively simple low-poly kit geometry**, not bespoke sculptural/exclusive Bastionfall art. The keep is partly out of gameplay-camera view near the scene edge; the courtyard, terrain grass, vegetation, riverbanks and rocks are still sparse/repetitive, and the HUD is still a large diagnostic overlay. Character silhouette, attack recoil/foot plants and atmospheric grounding need substantial improvement. The gold/blue pads communicate placement but their texture and silhouette hierarchy still need more imaginative authored shaping and detail. **No claim of Kingdom Rush graphical parity, user art signoff, GPU frame-time or real-device/mobile FPS.**
