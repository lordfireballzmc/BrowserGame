## What the game actually does

Starting wallet **25 copper crowns** from `BALANCE.economy.startingCrowns` (not a simulated counter), immutable integer ledger for earned/spent/recovery pot. A raid enemy defeated by a real `hp=0` transition with valid world `hitTick` yields a seeded once-only **Scrapper 2–3 or Runner 2** drop. It appears as a small physical world-space copper/octagonal 3D coin after 0.15s and must be reached by actual Warden within **1.7 m**; no credit for each shot and no double payment from the persistent corpse. At 22s uncollected value moves into a tracked **future half-value recovery pot, not instantly into the wallet**; pot collection is deliberately unfinished until full wave recovery. `tests/defense-economy.test.ts` verifies mature pickup timing/proximity, once-only rewards, expiration/ledger and deterministic replay.

P01/P02 are **actual wave-one unlocked pads**: buy a Bolt Spire for **20 crowns** through visible focusable game buttons or numeric keys `1`/`2`. Build requests validate exact authored pad/unlock and prevent duplicate, invalid or insufficient purchases. A purchased tower appears as the *existing original stone-and-timber `BoltSpireStudy` 3D mesh* in the same Three color/depth/shadow scene, with real 0.9s cooldown, **9.5m radius, 15m/s physically traveling 3D projectile and 13 true HP damage** on a moving-target swept collision, never instant damage at launch. Tower and coin visuals are GPU/draw-call-budget-bound regular gameplay render instances, not billboard HUD-only placeholders. The existing 258 scenery locations, ten pads, coast/road, hero and raider physics, source assets and original Warden bolt game logic are unchanged.
